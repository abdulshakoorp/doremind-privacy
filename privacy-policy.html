Privacy Policy

DoRemind · Last updated: 4 November 2026

In short: DoRemind has no accounts, no sign-in, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Your tasks, notes, budget data, and attachments stay on your device. The developer cannot see your data.

This policy explains what information DoRemind (the "App") handles and how. It applies to the Android and iOS versions of the App published by abdulshakoorp (the "Developer").

1. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following information only on your device, in its private local database and app storage:

  • Tasks: titles, descriptions, due date and time, priority, status (pending or completed), tags, and subtasks.
  • Reminders and history: scheduled reminder times, nag-mode settings, and a history of reminders that have fired.
  • Attachments: files you choose to attach to a task. They are copied into the App's private storage.
  • Notes and note folders that you create.
  • Budget data: monthly budgets and expenses that you enter.
  • App settings: such as theme, font size, and nag interval. Some of these are also cached in the App's local web storage so the screen loads with your chosen theme.

The App does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or location.

2. Information the App does not collect or share

  • The Developer does not operate a server that receives your tasks, notes, budgets, or attachments.
  • The App contains no third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs.
  • The App does not sell, rent, or share your personal information.
  • The App does not track you across other apps or websites.

3. Permissions and why they are used

The App asks for the following permissions. You can change them at any time in your device's settings.

Permission Purpose When it is used
Notifications Show your task reminders. Requested so reminders can appear.
Exact alarms (Android) Fire reminders at the exact due time, even when the App is closed. Requested when needed so reminders are not delayed.
Run at startup (Android) Re-create your scheduled reminders after the device restarts. Used automatically after a restart.
Keep device awake (Android) Lets the system deliver a scheduled reminder reliably. Used briefly when a reminder is delivered.
Microphone Voice input for entering or dictating task details. Only when you use voice input.
Calendar Lets the app copy your pending tasks to a calendar named "DoRemind" on your device. Only when you tap "Sync to device calendar" on the Calendar screen.
Internet Used by your device's speech recognition service during voice input. The App does not send your task data over the internet. Only during voice input.

4. Voice input

When you use voice input, the App asks your device's built-in speech recognition service to convert your speech to text. The App does not record or store audio. Depending on your device and its settings, the speech service may process audio on the device or send it to its provider (for example, Google on Android). That provider's privacy policy governs that processing.

5. Calendar sync

Calendar sync is manual. When you tap "Sync to device calendar" on the Calendar screen, the App lists the calendars on your device to find one named "DoRemind" (or creates it), then adds one event for each pending task. Each tap adds the events again. The App does not update or delete events it has already added, and it does not read events from your other calendars. If the DoRemind calendar belongs to an online account, such as a Google account, those events are stored in that account under the provider's privacy policy. The Developer does not receive these events.

6. Backup and data transfer

The App is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so your App data is not copied to your Google account backup. There is no built-in cloud backup in the App.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual tasks, notes, expenses, attachments, and notification history inside the App.
  • Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all of its data from your device. This cannot be undone, and the App cannot recover it.
  • You can also clear the App's data from your device's app settings. This deletes all of its data but keeps the App installed.

8. Security

Your data is protected by your device's security features and the operating system's app sandbox, which prevents other apps from reading it. The local database is not separately encrypted by the App, so we recommend keeping your device locked with a PIN, password, or biometric lock.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and the Developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App collects no personal information from any user.

10. Your choices

  • Revoke any permission in your device's settings. Reminders may not fire if you revoke notifications or exact alarms.
  • Do not tap "Sync to device calendar" if you do not want tasks copied to your calendar. To remove copied events, delete the "DoRemind" calendar in your device's calendar app.
  • Avoid voice input if you do not want to use your device's speech recognition service.
  • Uninstall the App to remove all of its data.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
